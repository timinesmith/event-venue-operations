# Event Staffing Model

**Status:** Illustrative demonstration. Ratios must be adapted to venue policy, event type, licensing requirements, and local rules.

## Planning inputs

- Expected attendance and peak arrival period
- Number of entrances, bars, seating areas, and backstage zones
- Event duration plus load-in and load-out windows
- Experience level and supervisory coverage
- Accessibility and guest-service needs

## Role structure

| Area | Core roles | Planning note |
|---|---|---|
| Leadership | Event lead, finance/settlement lead | One accountable lead per function |
| Guest access | Door lead, ticket support, ushers | Staff for peak, not average, arrival |
| Food and beverage | Manager, cashiers, runners | Align breaks with service peaks |
| Production | Stage manager, audio/lighting support, runners | Confirm call times in the production schedule |
| Safety and support | Security lead, medical liaison, facilities support | Follow venue and jurisdictional requirements |

## Control points

1. Roster draft due five business days before the event.
2. Final roster locked 48 hours before doors unless the event lead approves a change.
3. Actual hours captured the same night for settlement and payroll review.
4. Staffing variance explained in the post-event report.
