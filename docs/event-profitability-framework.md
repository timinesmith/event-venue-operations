# Event Profitability Framework

**Status:** Illustrative demonstration. No actual event or venue figures are included.

## Review stages

### Before contracting

- Confirm revenue sources and who controls each source.
- Identify guarantees, variable costs, staffing minimums, and cancellation exposure.
- Separate direct event costs from allocated facility costs.
- Record assumptions that would change the decision if wrong.

### Before doors

- Lock the staffing roster and vendor orders.
- Compare current forecast with the approved budget.
- Flag any cost that lacks a contract, purchase order, or approval.

### Settlement

- Reconcile ticket, POS, rental, and sponsorship revenue.
- Match labor and vendor charges to approved schedules and invoices.
- Document deductions and disputed items separately.
- Obtain preparer and reviewer sign-off.

### Post-event review

- Explain the largest favorable and unfavorable variances.
- Record avoidable costs and process failures.
- Update planning rates for future events.
