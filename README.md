# Event & Venue Operations

## Executive summary

Operational and financial frameworks for live events: profitability analysis, settlement preparation, staffing, production scheduling, and vendor coordination.

## Business problem

Event teams work under fixed show dates while costs, staffing, vendors, and last-minute production changes continue to move. Without a shared settlement and readiness system, small exceptions become expensive surprises after the event.

## Scope

- Event P&L and settlement framework
- Staffing model
- Production-day schedule
- Vendor coordination checklist
- Post-event review controls

## Role and evidence basis

**Illustrative demonstration informed by verified experience.** Timothy supported financial and administrative execution for 150+ concerts, private events, and related programs in approximately five months at a high-volume live music venue. His work included budget tracking, AP/AR, payroll coordination, vendor onboarding, ticket audits, artist settlement reporting, and expense reconciliations.

No internal venue data, contracts, artist terms, employee records, or proprietary files are included.

## Methods and tools

Event P&L design, settlement review, staffing ratios, run-of-show coordination, vendor checklists, Excel reporting, Paycor, Toast POS, and Compeat Advantage concepts.
