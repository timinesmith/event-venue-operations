# Vendor Coordination Checklist

**Status:** Illustrative demonstration.

## Before approval

- [ ] Scope and deliverables written in plain language
- [ ] Date, call time, load-in path, and contact role confirmed
- [ ] Price, taxes, overtime triggers, and cancellation terms documented
- [ ] Insurance or credential requirements confirmed by the venue
- [ ] Invoice and payment instructions collected

## Before the event

- [ ] Purchase order or written approval issued
- [ ] Delivery window confirmed 72 hours and 24 hours in advance
- [ ] Site access and parking/load-in instructions sent
- [ ] Backup contact identified
- [ ] Change-order approver named

## After the event

- [ ] Service completion confirmed by event lead
- [ ] Invoice matched to approval and actual service
- [ ] Damage, shortage, or overage documented
- [ ] Payment status entered in AP tracker
- [ ] Vendor performance note recorded
